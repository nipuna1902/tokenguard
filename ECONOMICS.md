# ECONOMICS.md

TokenGuard is fundamentally a B2B lead-generation and infrastructure-finance qualification tool rather than a standalone SaaS business. Its value comes from identifying startups with meaningful AI infrastructure spend and converting a subset of them into higher-value Credex financial relationships.

The first important assumption is estimating what a converted lead is worth. Suppose an average qualified startup customer eventually moves approximately $20,000/month of infrastructure or AI-related spend through Credex-supported financial products. If Credex captures even a modest 2–4% effective revenue margin across financing, transaction fees, or credit products, the annualized value of that customer could reasonably fall between $5,000–$10,000 ARR.

That means even relatively low conversion rates can justify significant top-of-funnel acquisition efforts.

The acquisition strategy intentionally relies on low-cost founder-driven distribution:

- Hacker News,
- Twitter/X,
- startup communities,
- engineering-focused Reddit threads,
- and direct founder outreach.

Because these channels are primarily organic, estimated CAC is extremely low initially:

- Twitter/X organic posts: effectively $0–$10 CAC
- Founder DMs and manual outreach: ~$20–$40 CAC in time cost
- Hacker News exposure: potentially near-zero marginal CAC
- Community partnerships/newsletters: ~$50–$100 CAC if scaled later

Now consider a simplified conversion funnel.

Suppose:

- 1,000 visitors land on TokenGuard
- 20% complete an audit → 200 audits
- 25% save reports → 50 leads
- 15% book consultations → ~7 calls
- 20% of calls convert → 1–2 customers

If one converted customer is worth approximately $7,500 ARR, then:

- 1 customer from 1,000 visitors already creates meaningful ROI
- especially because acquisition costs remain relatively low in the early stages

For TokenGuard to contribute toward $1M ARR in 18 months, the business would likely need:

- roughly 130–150 equivalent converted customers at ~$7k ARR each,
- or fewer higher-value infrastructure finance relationships.

Operationally, that could mean:

- ~15k–30k monthly visitors,
- strong founder/community distribution,
- and a reliable audit-to-consultation conversion pipeline.

The important insight is that TokenGuard does not need massive SaaS-style recurring subscription revenue itself. Its economic value comes from qualifying infrastructure-spend-heavy startups and routing them into higher-margin financial products and relationships that Credex already understands operationally.
